Make an archive

There is no archive here yet. arv keeps an archive's record in a .arv folder at the root of what it describes: the folder holding your photos and projects, or your NAS share. Nothing else in it is changed, and nothing in it is archived until you choose it.

What is owed, from arv todo: copies to make, copies to read back, cold copies, places, checks overdue. Each heading says what to do; a disc's name opens it, to record copies and places.

Everything kept, and where every copy of it is: each data object's versions and each collection's newest edition, the discs holding them, each copy (disc, image or folder; cold warm hot; ✓ read back) and whether the original is still on the PC/NAS.

Each collection and its editions: an edition is safe once every one of its discs has a copy read back identical; a newer safe edition replaces older ones, unless they are kept. Retiring records nothing until you confirm, and arv deletes nothing: the discs stay yours to keep or destroy.

Make a folder a collection

A folder you keep sorting: each time you make discs of it, they are its next edition. arv writes a small .arv marker in it, and nothing else.
